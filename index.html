Our story

Born from two pet parents with too many cute ideas.

GMerch started with one impossible mission: make pet merch feel as joyful as the pets wearing it. We wanted fewer boring basics and more soft colors, plush textures, happy details, and clothes that make strangers stop and say, “Wait… where did you get that?”

Now our little pastel wonderland is filled with playful looks for dogs and cats who deserve compliments, cuddles, and a tiny bit of drama.
