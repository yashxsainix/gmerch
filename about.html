About GMerch

Built by pet parents who wanted every wag and purr to feel extra special.

GMerch was founded in NYC by a group of friends who are pet parents and loved dressing their pets but never found merch that felt playful enough, soft enough, or photo-ready enough. So they made their own pastel dream world.

Our story

From one pet birthday outfit to a full-on pastel pet universe.

It started with a rescue pup named Mochi and a cat named Miso. Their humans wanted to find pieces that felt whimsical without being stiff or scratchy, charming without feeling costume-y, and premium enough to gift. That search turned into sketchbooks full of cloud-soft hoodies, sparkle collars, and bandanas that looked like candy wrappers from a dream.

Friends began asking where the outfits came from. Then neighbors did. Soon, every little test design turned into a mini sidewalk photo shoot. GMerch grew from those happy moments: pets feeling comfy, people smiling at them, and everyday routines becoming softer and more magical.

Today, the brand is still guided by the same idea that started it all: pets deserve merch that celebrates their personality, keeps them comfortable, and makes pet parents grin the second they open the package.
