Founded right here in NYC by pet parents who believed our furry friends deserve better than boring beige collars.

The GMerch Mission 🚀

It all started in a tiny New York apartment. We were tired of seeing the same dull, uninspired pet accessories. We wanted pastels! We wanted personality! We wanted our pets to look as fabulous as they make us feel. So, we designed our first collection of ultra-cute, Instagram-worthy pet merch.
